## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The workhorse CTA, rendered in {colors.primary} (#262626) with white text and {rounded.sm} corners. Uppercase label in Chalet at 14px/600 weight. On hover, shifts to {colors.primary-active} (#0b0b0b). Disabled state uses {colors.muted-soft} (#b0b0b0) on {colors.canvas} (#f4f4f6). **`button-secondary`** — Outlined variant on {colors.canvas} background with a 1px {colors.hairline} border. Active state darkens the border to {colors.muted}. **`button-accent-pink`** — Uses the brand's signature {colors.accent-pink} (#feb7bb) with dark ink text, used for promotional CTAs and limited-edition drops. **`button-accent-marigold`** — The {colors.accent-marigold} (#ffcf2a) variant, reserved for sale events and high-visibility actions. **`button-ghost`** — Text-only button with no background or border, used for secondary actions like "View Details" or "Learn More." Hover state adds a subtle underline.

### Navigation
**`nav-bar`** — A fixed or sticky 72px bar on {colors.canvas} with a soft bottom border ({colors.hairline-soft}). Navigation links are uppercase 13px/600 in Chalet. Active link gets a 2px bottom border in {colors.primary}. Inactive links render in {colors.muted}. The bar contains the Bala wordmark (left), category links (center), and utility icons (search, account, cart) on the right. On mobile, the category links collapse into a hamburger menu. **`nav-link-active`** — The active state uses a 2px solid underline in {colors.primary} (#262626). **`nav-link-inactive`** — Muted gray (#878787) with no underline, transitions to {colors.ink} on hover.

### Cards
**`product-card`** — A clean white card with {rounded.sm} corners and no padding at the container level. The product image fills the top with a 1:1 aspect ratio and rounded top corners. Below, a padded info area ({spacing.base} on sides, {spacing.lg} at bottom) holds the product title in {typography.title-sm} and the price in {typography.price}. A **`product-card-badge`** may appear overlaid on the image, using {colors.accent-pink} background with uppercase 11px/700 text. Cards have no shadow by default; on hover, a subtle 0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08) shadow lifts the card.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Nav bar collapses to hamburger menu; product cards go single-column (1 col); hero typography drops to {typography.display-md} (28px); footer columns stack vertically; color swatches reduce to 28px; quantity selector becomes full-width; search bar moves to a slide-out panel |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Nav links remain visible but condensed; product cards display in 2-column grid; hero typography at {typography.display-lg} (36px); footer in 2-column layout; search bar remains in nav but shrinks width |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Full nav with all links; product cards in 3-column grid; hero at full {typography.display-xl} (48px); footer in 4-column layout; search bar at full width in nav |
| Wide | > 1440px | Max-width container at 1440px with centered content; product cards may expand to 4-column grid; hero remains at max width with increased padding |

### Touch Targets
- All interactive elements (buttons, links, inputs) maintain a minimum 44px height for touch accessibility.
- Nav links have a minimum 48px tap area on mobile.
- Color swatches are 32px with a 44px tap target via transparent padding.
- Quantity selector +/- buttons are 44px wide.
- Accordion headers have a minimum 48px tap height.

### Collapsing Strategy
- On mobile (< 744px), the main navigation collapses into a hamburger menu that slides in from the left.
- The search bar collapses into a search icon that opens a full-screen overlay on mobile.
- Product filters collapse into a "Filter" button that opens a bottom sheet on mobile.
- Footer columns collapse from 4 to 2 on tablet, and to a single stacked column on mobile.
- Accordion sections are collapsed by default on all breakpoints, expanding on click.
