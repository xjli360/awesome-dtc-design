Battle Beaver doesn't do quiet; it does neon on void, blocky sans on black, and corners only where a thumb needs to land.

## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The primary call-to-action across the site, rendered in electric blue (#0099ff) with white uppercase Arial Black text. Used for "Add to Cart", "Start Configuring", and "Checkout" flows. On hover, shifts to `{colors.primary-active}` (#007acc). Disabled state uses `{colors.primary-disabled}` (#4da6ff) with reduced opacity. All primary buttons maintain a consistent 44px height with 12px 24px padding and `{rounded.sm}` corners.

**`button-secondary`** — An outlined variant with transparent background and a 2px solid `{colors.primary}` border. Text remains the same electric blue. Used for "Learn More", "View Details", and secondary configurator actions. Active state shifts the border to `{colors.primary-active}`.

**`button-accent-mint`** — A high-energy alternative using `{colors.accent-mint}` (#53ffba) background with dark (#121212) text. Used for "Pro Upgrade", "Limited Edition", and premium upsells. The mint-on-black combo signals performance tier.

**`button-accent-orange`** — Uses `{colors.accent-orange}` (#ff8c00) for urgency-driven CTAs like "Limited Stock" and "Flash Sale". White text, same structural specs.

**`button-accent-hotpink`** — Uses `{colors.accent-hotpink}` (#ff1493) for "Custom Shop" and "Design Your Own" entry points. White text.

**`button-pill`** — A fully rounded variant (`{rounded.full}`) at 36px height with smaller uppercase text. Used in the configurator for option toggles, filter chips, and quick-select actions. Available in primary blue and accent-mint variants.

**`button-tertiary-text`** — A text-only button with no background or border. Uses `{colors.primary}` for text. Used for "Cancel", "Back", and inline navigation within the configurator wizard.

### Cards
**`product-card`** — A sharp-cornered (`{rounded.none}`) card on `{colors.surface-card}` (#2a2a2a) background. Contains a 1:1 aspect ratio product image, title in `{typography.title-sm}`, price in `{colors.accent-mint}`, and optional badge overlay. On hover, a 1px `{colors.primary}` border appears. No shadow — the brand relies on contrast, not elevation.

**`product-card-badge`** — Overlaid on the top-left of product images. Uses `{colors.badge-pro}` (#ff8c00) background with dark text. Tight padding (2px 8px) with `{rounded.xs}` corners. Variants exist for "LIMITED" (hotpink) and "CUSTOM" (mint).

### Navigation
**`top-nav`** — A fixed 64px bar on `{colors.canvas}` (#121212) with a subtle bottom border (`{colors.hairline}`). Navigation links use `{typography.nav-link}` — 13px uppercase Arial Black with 1px letter spacing. Active links glow `{colors.primary}`, inactive links sit at `{colors.muted-soft}` (#a2a2a2). The logo sits left, cart icon right.

**`search-bar`** — A pill-shaped input (`{rounded.full}`) at 40px height on `{colors.surface-soft}` (#1f1f1f) with a 1px `{colors.hairline}` border. On focus, the border thickens to 2px `{colors.primary}`. Placeholder text in `{colors.muted}` (#565656).

### Forms & Inputs
**`text-input`** — Standard form input at 44px height with `{rounded.sm}` corners. Background is `{colors.surface-soft}`, border is 1px `{colors.hairline}`. On focus, border switches to 2px `{colors.primary}`. Used in checkout forms, account pages, and contact forms.

**`select-dropdown`** — Matches `text-input` structure but includes a custom dropdown arrow. Used for controller model selection, region, and sorting options.

**`quantity-selector`** — A compact 44px height input with `{rounded.sm}` corners. Used in cart and product detail pages. Includes minus/plus buttons on either side.

### Configurator
**`configurator-step`** — The main content area for each step of the controller customization wizard. Uses `{colors.surface-soft}` background with `{rounded.sm}` corners and `{spacing.lg}` padding. Title in `{typography.title-md}`.

**`configurator-option-pill`** — Individual option buttons within the configurator. Default state is `{colors.surface-card}` with `{colors.muted-soft}` text and a 1px `{colors.hairline}` border. Selected state flips to `{colors.primary}` background with white text. Always `{rounded.full}` at 36px height.

**`configurator-swatch`** — A 32px circle (`{rounded.full}`) representing a color/finish option. Selected state adds a 2px white border. Used in grids for shell colors, button colors, thumbstick colors, and grip finishes.

**`configurator-3d-preview`** — The live 3D controller render area. Background is `{colors.canvas}` (#121212) with `{rounded.sm}` corners. Updates in real-time as options change.

### Footer
**`footer-section`** — A full-width section on `{colors.surface-soft}` (#1f1f1f) with `{spacing.section}` vertical padding. Links use `{typography.link}` in `{colors.muted-soft}` (#a2a2a2), hovering to `{colors.primary}`. Social icons are 32px circles with `{rounded.full}`, defaulting to `{colors.muted-soft}` and shifting to `{colors.primary}` on hover.

### Badges
**`badge-pro`** — Orange (#ff8c00) background, dark text. Used for "PRO" tier controllers and components.
**`badge-limited`** — Hotpink (#ff1493) background, white text. Used for limited edition drops and time-sensitive offers.
**`badge-custom`** — Mint (#53ffba) background, dark text. Used for fully custom configurations.

### Cart & Checkout
**`cart-item`** — A sharp-cornered row on `{colors.surface-card}` with a bottom border. Contains product thumbnail, title, quantity selector, and price in `{colors.accent-mint}`.

**`checkout-button`** — The final purchase CTA, rendered in `{colors.accent-mint}` (#53ffba) with dark text. Larger than standard buttons at 48px height with 14px 24px padding. Active state shifts to `{colors.accent-mint-active}` (#00e19c).

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grid; configurator becomes full-screen modal; top-nav collapses to hamburger; badges stack vertically; search bar hidden behind icon |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; configurator sidebar collapses to top tabs; top-nav shows 4 primary links; search bar expands on tap |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three-column product grid; configurator shows persistent sidebar with step progress; full top-nav with all links; search bar always visible |
| Wide | > 1440px | Four-column product grid; configurator sidebar expands with additional option groups; max-width container at 1440px |

### Touch Targets
- All buttons and interactive elements maintain minimum 44px height (exceeding Apple's 44pt guideline)
- Configurator option pills are 36px height — acceptable for desktop, but mobile uses 44px variants
- Swatch circles are 32px — borderline for touch; mobile increases to 40px
- Cart quantity selector maintains 44px height across all breakpoints
- Navigation links have 48px minimum tap area on mobile

### Collapsing Strategy
- Top-nav: On mobile, full link list collapses into a hamburger menu with slide-out drawer
- Configurator: On mobile, the multi-step sidebar collapses into a single-column vertical flow with progress dots
- Product filters: On tablet and below, filter sidebar collapses into a bottom sheet or modal
- Footer: On mobile, multi-column footer collapses into stacked accordion sections
- Search: On mobile, search bar collapses to an icon that expands to full-width overlay on tap
