## Components

### Buttons

**`button-primary`** — A solid burnt-copper rectangle with barely rounded corners (`{rounded.xs}`), white text in weight 600. On hover it deepens to `{colors.primary-active}`, a darker roast that maintains contrast. Disabled state washes to a sandy `{colors.primary-disabled}`. The generous 52px height accommodates touch targets without looking bloated against large product imagery.

**`button-secondary`** — White fill with a 2px ink border and ink-colored label. On hover, the fill inverts to full ink with white text — a confident toggle that signals a secondary but equally valid path (e.g., "Compare Models" alongside "Add to Cart").

**`button-add-to-cart`** — Full-width variant of the primary button at 56px height, used exclusively on product detail pages. The extra 4px of height and wider padding distinguish it as the page's single conversion action.

**`button-tertiary`** — A text-only link style with a 2px underline in `{colors.primary}`. Used for inline actions like "View Specifications" or "See All Reviews" where a full button would over-compete with the primary CTA.

### Navigation

**`nav-bar`** — A 64px-tall dark bar in `{colors.nav-dark}` (#121212) spanning full viewport width. Logo sits left, navigation links center, cart/account icons right. Dropdown menus emerge below in white cards with subtle shadow, listing product categories (Wood-Fired, Gas, Hybrid) with thumbnail images.

**`announcement-bar`** — A 40px ribbon above the nav in `{colors.primary}` carrying promotional messaging (free shipping thresholds, seasonal sales) in white caption text. Dismissable via an × icon on the right edge.

**`breadcrumb`** — Muted text crumbs separated by chevrons, sitting between nav and page content with `{spacing.lg}` vertical breathing room. The final crumb renders in `{colors.ink}` to indicate current location.

### Product Display

**`product-card`** — White card with a 1:1 aspect-ratio image container (object-fit: contain against `{colors.surface-soft}` background), ensuring ovens of different proportions align in grid. Below the image: title in `{typography.title-sm}`, price in `{typography.price-md}`, and an optional badge positioned absolutely at top-left of the image. On hover the border strengthens and a soft shadow lifts the card.

**`product-card-image`** — The image area uses `{colors.surface-soft}` as its background so that product shots on transparent or white backgrounds still read as contained within the card boundary.

**`gallery-thumbnail`** — Small 64×64 image tiles below the main product image. Active thumbnail gains a 2px `{colors.primary}` border; inactive ones show no border. Clicking swaps the hero image.

### Selectors

**`fuel-type-selector`** — Pill-style toggle buttons for choosing between Wood-Fired, Gas, and Hybrid fuel types. Active state gets a `{colors.primary}` border and retains the white background; inactive shows `{colors.hairline}` border. Each option may include a small icon (flame, gas burner, dual icon).

**`size-selector`** — Similar toggle pattern for oven diameter (750, 500, etc.). Active selection highlighted with `{colors.primary}` border. Typography is slightly heavier (`{typography.title-sm}`) to give the size number prominence.

### Specification & Comparison

**`spec-table`** — A soft-gray panel listing key–value pairs (cooking area, weight, max temperature, fuel type). Labels are uppercased in `{typography.spec-label}` with muted color; values are `{typography.body-sm}` in `{colors.ink}`. Alternating rows separated by a 1px `{colors.hairline-soft}` line.

**`comparison-grid`** — A multi-column layout for side-by-side oven comparison. Each column is a bordered card showing model image, key specs, price, and a primary CTA. Sticky headers keep model names visible on scroll.

### Social Proof

**`testimonial-card`** — Warm-toned card (`{colors.surface-warm}`) with large body text for the quote, customer name in `{typography.title-sm}`, and a row of star icons in `{colors.star-rating}`. Cards stack in a horizontal carousel on mobile, a 3-up grid on desktop.

**`badge-sale`** / **`badge-bestseller`** / **`badge-in-stock`** — Small uppercased labels with tight padding. Sale badges use `{colors.error}` red for urgency, bestseller uses `{colors.primary}` copper, and in-stock uses `{colors.success}` green.

### Hero

**`hero-banner`** — Full-bleed section with a background image of an oven in use (flames visible, outdoor setting). A left-weighted gradient overlay ensures headline legibility. Display text in `{typography.display-xl}` white, sub-headline in `{typography.body-lg}`, and a `button-primary` CTA below. Minimum height 560px ensures cinematic presence.

### Footer

**`footer`** — Dark charcoal (`{colors.charcoal}`) block with four-column link layout: Shop, Support, About, Connect. Links render in `{colors.hairline}` and brighten to white on hover. A bottom row carries copyright, payment icons, and legal links separated by pipes.

### Search

**`search-overlay`** — A centered modal card triggered by the nav search icon. White background, rounded corners, prominent text input at top. Below the input, recent searches and popular products appear as suggestions with thumbnail images.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grid; hero text drops to `{typography.display-md}`; nav collapses to hamburger + slide-out drawer; fuel/size selectors stack vertically; spec table scrolls horizontally; footer stacks to single column |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; hero maintains full bleed but at 400px min-height; nav shows top-level links, dropdowns remain; comparison grid scrolls horizontally with 2 visible columns |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three-column product grid; full nav with mega-dropdown; hero at 560px; comparison grid shows 3 columns without scroll; spec table renders inline beside product gallery |
| Wide | > 1440px | Content max-width caps at 1440px and centers; product grid may expand to 4 columns; hero image scales proportionally; generous `{spacing.section}` padding increases by 25% |

### Touch Targets

- All interactive elements maintain 44×44px minimum touch area on mobile
- Fuel-type and size selectors expand to full-width stacked buttons below 744px
- Gallery thumbnails increase to 72×72px on touch devices with 8px gap
- Nav hamburger icon has 48px tap target with visual size of 24px

### Collapsing Strategy

- Mega-nav → hamburger drawer with accordion sub-menus at < 744px
- Product comparison grid → horizontal scroll carousel at < 1128px
- Spec table → stacked key-value pairs at < 744px
- Footer 4-column → 2-column at tablet, single accordion column at mobile
- Announcement bar text truncates with ellipsis at < 480px, full text at wider
