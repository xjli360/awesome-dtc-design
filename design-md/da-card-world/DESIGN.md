## Components

### Buttons

**`button-primary`** — The primary CTA uses `{colors.primary}` (#e02020 inferred red) on a dark canvas, rendering as the sole high-contrast warm element on any product or checkout page. Height is fixed at 40px with `{rounded.xs}` corners — no pills, no heavy radii, keeping the warehouse-catalog register intact. Hover darkens to `{colors.primary-active}`; disabled washes to `{colors.primary-disabled}` to signal unavailability without removing the button from layout flow.

**`button-secondary`** — `{colors.surface-raised}` fill with a `{colors.hairline}` border; sits beside primary on product pages for actions like "Add to Watchlist." Shares `{rounded.xs}` so button pairs are visually consistent.

**`button-ghost`** — Transparent fill, `{colors.hairline}` border; used for tertiary actions (share, print invoice, view grading details) where de-emphasis is important and the dark canvas should bleed through.

### Search Bar

**`search-bar`** — The most structurally important component on the site. Rendered as a wide `{colors.surface-soft}` field spanning the majority of the header width on desktop, with a magnifier icon in `{colors.muted}`. On focus, border switches to `{colors.link}` blue to indicate active state. The companion `search-results-dropdown` renders with a heavy `rgba(0,0,0,0.6)` shadow against the dark canvas, displaying card name, set, and grade matches as the user types.

### Navigation

**`nav-bar-top`** — A 32px utility strip above the main nav in `{colors.canvas-deep}` carrying account links, cart, and shipping notices in `{typography.caption}`. **`nav-bar`** — 52px main nav with sport/category dropdowns. `{typography.nav-link}` at weight 500 keeps the label density readable across deep category hierarchies (Sport → League → Brand → Year → Set).

### Product Card

**`product-card`** — The workhorse grid unit. `{colors.surface-card}` background with a `{colors.canvas-deep}` image well isolates card photography. Title in `{typography.body-sm}`, price prominent in `{typography.price-md}`. A `grade-badge` overlays the image corner when the item is a graded slab. On hover, border brightens to `{colors.muted}` and card lifts 2px — a subtle elevation signal without dramatic animation.

### Grade Badges

**`grade-badge`** — Compact rectangular badges rendered in the registrar's house color: PSA items get `{colors.badge-psa}` red, BGS items get `{colors.badge-bgs}` blue, SGC gets `{colors.badge-sgc}` orange, CGC gets `{colors.badge-cgc}` purple. Grade score (e.g. "10", "9.5") renders in `{typography.grade-score}` at weight 800 — the highest typographic weight on the page — since grade denominations are the primary purchase signal for collectors.

### Hero Banner

**`hero-banner`** — Full-bleed image zones with a `rgba(30,30,30,0.65)` scrim, allowing product photography to read while overlaid title text in `{typography.display-xl}` maintains legibility. Used for box break announcements, release day promotions, and season openers. Minimum height 340px to give large card images adequate field.

### Filters & Sorting

**`filter-sidebar`** — Left-rail on catalog pages; `{colors.surface-card}` with section headers in `{typography.title-sm}`. Checkbox filters use `{colors.primary}` accent on selection. **`sort-dropdown`** — compact 36px control anchored above the grid allowing relevance/price/grade sorting without consuming vertical space.

### Footer

**`footer`** — `{colors.canvas-deep}` base, link columns in `{typography.body-sm}`, section headers in `{typography.title-sm}` at `{colors.body}`. Links render in `{colors.link}` blue for discoverability. Newsletter input re-uses `text-input` styles with an inline submit button in `{colors.primary}`.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grid; nav collapses to hamburger; search bar moves below logo row; filter sidebar becomes full-height drawer |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; nav shows top-level categories with overflow menu; filter sidebar toggles as overlay panel |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three- to four-column product grid; full horizontal nav with category dropdowns; filter sidebar fixed left rail at ~220px |
| Wide | > 1440px | Grid scales to five columns; max content width ~1440px with balanced side margins |

### Touch Targets

- All buttons minimum 40px height; `button-primary` and `button-secondary` meet 40px minimum
- Grade badges are display-only but card tap target covers the entire `product-card` tile
- Nav items on mobile minimum 44px touch height via padding expansion
- Pagination controls minimum 36×36px per page button

### Collapsing Strategy

- Category navigation: desktop dropdown mega-menu → tablet accordion within hamburger → mobile full-screen drawer
- Filter sidebar: desktop persistent left rail → tablet slide-in overlay → mobile bottom-sheet drawer
- Product grid: 5-col → 4-col → 3-col → 2-col → 1-col across breakpoints
- Hero banner: text/image side-by-side on desktop → stacked with image below on mobile; scrim opacity increases on mobile for text contrast
- Search bar: full-width in header on desktop → full-width second row on mobile after logo/cart strip
