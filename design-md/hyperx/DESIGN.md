description: A high-voltage gaming hardware brand that lives in the tension between #c8202f and #232323 — a red so saturated it feels like a warning light, set against near-black backgrounds that absorb ambient light. The brand's visual system is built on aggressive contrast: #fafafa body text on #202020 surfaces, #d12442 accents that pulse like a controller's LED strip, and #ff8b21 used sparingly as a secondary voltage for limited-edition gear and promotional flares. Typography runs Proxima Nova at tight letter-spacing and bold weights — display headlines sit at 700 weight, 28px, with -0.5px tracking that compresses the wordforms into compact, impact-ready blocks. The signature design move is the "red accent line" — a 2px #c8202f horizontal rule that appears under navigation items, across product-card footers, and as the leading edge of every primary CTA. Product photography is always backlit or edge-lit, with the gear emerging from shadow rather than sitting on a white background, reinforcing the brand's "performance from darkness" ethos. Corner radii are minimal: 4px on buttons, 8px on cards — nothing pill-shaped, nothing playful. The brand trusts red and black to do the work of hierarchy, using #787878 and #969696 for secondary text and disabled states, and reserving #e6e6e6 for subtle dividers against the dark canvas. The Shopify checkout flow introduces #aabbaa as a neutral accent for payment buttons, a rare moment of calm in an otherwise high-contrast system.

## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The primary call-to-action across the entire site. Uses the brand's signature red (#c8202f) on a white label, with 4px corner radii that feel sharp and decisive. On hover, the red deepens to #a31a25; disabled state drops to #6b111a with #727272 text. The 48px height and 32px horizontal padding give it presence without overwhelming the layout. Always paired with uppercase button text in Proxima Nova Bold at 16px with 0.3px letter-spacing.

**`button-secondary`** — An outlined variant for secondary actions on dark backgrounds. Uses the surface-card color (#232323) as background with a 2px #3c3c3c border. On hover, the border switches to the primary red, creating a subtle voltage increase. Disabled state keeps the border but grays out the text. Same dimensions as primary for consistent alignment.

**`button-tertiary`** — A text-only button for less prominent actions like "Learn More" or "View Details." Transparent background with red text, no border. On hover, the text shifts to the active red. Used primarily within product cards and content sections where a full button would compete with the primary CTA.

**`button-ghost`** — A minimal button for utility actions (close, cancel, dismiss). Transparent background with white body text. On hover, gains a #3c3c3c background. No border, no red — designed to recede until needed.

**`button-pill-accent`** — A rare pill-shaped button reserved for promotional or limited-edition content. Uses the orange accent (#ff8b21) on dark text (#232323), creating a high-energy contrast that breaks the usual red-black system. Only appears on hero banners and special collection pages.

### Cards
**`product-card`** — The primary product display unit. A dark card (#232323) with 8px rounded corners, containing a product image against a true black (#202020) background, product name in body-sm, and price in the price token. On hover, the card gains a 1px #3c3c3c border and the background shifts to #3c3c3c. The image area uses a top-rounded-only treatment (8px top, 0 bottom) to create a clear separation between photography and information.

**`product-card-badge`** — A small red label overlaid on the product image. Uses 4px corners, 2px/8px padding, and uppercase 11px bold text. Positioned top-left on the image area. Variants include orange for "NEW" and bright red for "LIMITED."

**`product-card-price`** — The current price in 20px bold Proxima Nova. When on sale, the price switches to red and a line-through original price appears in muted gray below it.

### Navigation
**`nav-bar`** — A fixed 64px bar in true black (#232323) with uppercase navigation links in 14px semibold Proxima Nova. Active links show a 2px red underline (#c8202f) and red text; inactive links are muted gray (#969696) and turn white on hover. The bar collapses to 56px on scroll. The brand logo sits left-aligned, cart icon right-aligned with a red badge for item count.

**`nav-link-active`** — The active navigation state. Red text with a 2px red bottom border that acts as the brand's signature accent line. No background change — the red underline is the sole indicator.

**`nav-link-inactive`** — The default navigation state. Muted gray text (#969696) with no underline. On hover, text shifts to white (#fafafa).

### Forms
**`text-input`** — A dark input field on surface-card background with a 1px #3c3c3c border. On focus, the border switches to red. Error state also uses red border but with an error icon. Disabled inputs use a softer background (#2d2d2d) and muted text. All inputs use 4px corners and 48px height for consistency with buttons.

**`select-input`** — Matches text-input styling but includes a custom dropdown arrow. Same height, padding, and border treatment.

**`checkbox`** and **`radio`** — Small interactive elements with 2px borders on dark backgrounds. Checked/selected state fills with red. Checkboxes use 4px corners; radios use full rounding.

**`toggle`** — A 24px tall pill-shaped toggle for settings and filters. Off state is #3c3c3c; on state is red (#c8202f).

### Hero
**`hero-section`** — A full-width, minimum 480px tall section with a dark background (#202020) and a semi-transparent black overlay (40% opacity) over the background image. The headline uses display-xl (28px, 700 weight) with tight letter-spacing. A primary CTA button sits below the headline, with an optional secondary outlined button for alternative actions.

### Footer
**`footer-section`** — A dark footer (#232323) with muted gray text (#787878) for links and white text for section headings. Links are 14px regular weight and turn white on hover. A thin red accent line (#c8202f) separates the footer from the main content area.

### Dividers and Accents
**`divider`** — A 1px line in #3c3c3c used to separate sections. A softer variant (#2c2c2e) is used for less prominent separations.

**`accent-line`** — A 2px red line (#c8202f) used as a signature design element. Appears under active navigation items, at the top of product cards, and as a section separator in the footer.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column layout; nav collapses to hamburger; product cards stack vertically; hero height reduces to 320px; buttons go full-width; search becomes icon-only |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; nav links truncate to key items; hero at 400px; side filters collapse to top bar |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Full nav with all links; three-column product grid; hero at 480px; persistent sidebar filters |
| Wide | > 1440px | Max-width container at 1440px; content centered; product grid expands to four columns; hero at 560px |

### Touch Targets
- All interactive elements (buttons, links, inputs) maintain a minimum 44px height for touch accessibility
- Icon buttons are 40px with 44px touch area via padding
- Product card tap targets are the entire card surface
- Navigation hamburger icon is 44x44px on mobile

### Collapsing Strategy
- Top navigation collapses to hamburger menu below 744px
- Product filters collapse to a "Filter" button that opens a modal on mobile
- Product image galleries collapse from thumbnail strip to swipeable dots
- Footer link columns collapse to accordion sections on mobile
- Search bar collapses to icon-only, expanding to full-width on tap
- Secondary navigation (category links) collapses to a horizontal scrollable strip

## Known Gaps

- **Hover states for all components** — Only primary and secondary button hover states were extracted; tertiary, ghost, and link hover states are inferred from common patterns
- **Error and success styling** — Form validation colors (error text, success green) were not present in the extracted palette; error state uses primary red as a fallback
- **Dark mode** — The entire site is already dark-themed; no light mode variant was detected
- **Sub-brand palettes** — HyperX has multiple product lines (Cloud, Pulsefire, Alloy, etc.) that may use distinct accent colors; only the core red-black system was extracted
- **Animation and transition values** — No timing functions or durations were extracted; standard 200-300ms ease-in-out assumed
- **Typography scale** — Font sizes and weights were estimated from extracted CSS; the exact type scale may vary across pages
- **Spacing scale** — The spacing tokens are based on common 4px/8px systems; the actual site may use a different base unit
- **Shopify checkout styling** — The #aabbaa color appears in checkout flows but its exact usage (payment buttons, backgrounds) is uncertain
- **Social media icon colors** — Extracted colors may include social platform brand colors (Facebook blue, Twitter blue, etc.) that are not part of the HyperX design system
- **Stock image dominant tones** — Some extracted grays may come from product photography rather than the design system itself
- **The extracted color list is heavily weighted toward grays and blacks** — The true brand palette likely has fewer neutral tones; the primary red (#c8202f) and accent orange (#ff8b21) are the most distinctive brand colors
