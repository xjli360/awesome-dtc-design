## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The primary call-to-action, rendered in the brand's deep brown `{colors.primary}` (#271f1f) with white text and an uppercase Brown typeface. On hover, it shifts to `{colors.primary-active}` (#5e5047) for a subtle lift, and when disabled it fades to `{colors.primary-disabled}` (#cccccc) with muted text. The `button-secondary` variant inverts the scheme with a white background, brown text, and a 2px solid border, ideal for ghosted actions like "Learn More" or "Add to Wishlist." Accent buttons (`button-accent-sun`, `button-accent-teal`) use the brand's signature yellow (#ffcf2a) or teal (#00caaa) for promotional or highlight moments, while `button-ghost` stays transparent for low-emphasis inline actions.

### Text Inputs & Forms
**`text-input`** — A clean, minimal input with a white background, 1px hairline border (#dedede), and 8px corner rounding. On focus, the border thickens to 2px and adopts the primary brown for clear visual feedback. Error states swap to `{colors.accent-coral}` (#ff5742) for immediate recognition. The `select-input` and `textarea` follow the same visual language, ensuring a cohesive form experience across the site. All inputs use Inter body text at 16px for readability.

### Navigation
**`nav-bar`** — A fixed-height 72px bar on a white canvas, with nav links set in Brown at 14px with 0.3px letter spacing. The active link is underlined with a 2px primary-brown border, while inactive links sit in `{colors.muted}` (#5e5047). On scroll, the bar shrinks to 64px and gains a subtle shadow for depth. The nav is designed to accommodate a central logo, left-aligned category links, and right-aligned utility icons (search, account, cart).

### Product Cards
**`product-card`** — The core shopping unit, a white card with a 1px soft hairline border and 12px rounding. The card image fills the top with a 1:1 aspect ratio, followed by the product title in `{typography.title-sm}`, price in muted text, and an optional star rating. On hover, the border strengthens and a shadow lifts the card. Badges (`product-card-badge` for "New" in coral, `product-card-badge-sale` for "Sale" in teal) are absolutely positioned over the image for immediate visual hierarchy.

### Hero Section
**`hero-section`** — A full-width banner with a soft background (`{colors.surface-soft}` #f7f7f8) and generous padding. The heading uses `{typography.display-xl}` (48px Brown bold) with a muted subheading beneath. A single primary CTA button anchors the composition. An accent variant (`hero-section-accent`) uses the brand's soft pink (#efe6e1) for seasonal or campaign hero blocks.

### Search
**`search-bar`** — A pill-shaped search input with full rounding, white background, and a 1px hairline border. On focus, the border becomes a 2px primary brown ring. The bar is designed to expand into a full overlay or dropdown on interaction, with recent searches and suggestions.

### Footer
**`footer-section`** — A dark footer anchored in `{colors.primary}` (#271f1f) with white text. Links are set in Inter at 14px and turn yellow (`{colors.accent-sun}`) on hover. The footer includes a newsletter signup with a white input and a yellow submit button, plus accordion-style mobile navigation for brand, support, and social links.

### Accordion
**`accordion-item`** — Used for product details, FAQ, and mobile navigation. Each item has a bottom border, a trigger in `{typography.title-sm}`, and expandable content in `{typography.body-sm}`. The trigger toggles with a simple rotate animation on a chevron icon.

### Tabs
**`tab-active`** and **`tab-inactive`** — Underlined tabs for product detail pages (e.g., "Description," "Ingredients," "How to Use"). The active tab has a 2px primary-brown underline and bold text, while inactive tabs are muted and borderless.

### Color Swatches
**`color-swatch`** — A 32px circular swatch with a 2px hairline border. When selected, the border switches to primary brown for clear selection state. Used extensively in product cards and PDPs for shade selection.

### Quantity Selector
**`quantity-selector`** — A compact horizontal control with minus/plus buttons flanking a numeric display. The container has a 1px hairline border and 8px rounding, while the buttons are transparent with primary-brown text.

### Star Rating
**`star-rating`** — A row of 16px star icons in `{colors.star-rating}` (#ffcf2a), with 2px gaps. Used on product cards and review sections to convey customer satisfaction at a glance.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column layout; nav collapses to hamburger; product cards stack full-width; hero padding reduces to 32px; footer accordions expand; search bar becomes full-width; typography scales down (display-xl becomes 32px, display-lg becomes 28px) |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; nav links remain visible but condensed; hero uses 2/3 + 1/3 split; side-by-side form fields; footer columns stack to 2x2 |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three-column product grid; full nav bar with all links; hero uses full-width imagery; multi-column footer; search bar in nav; product detail page uses 50/50 image + info split |
| Wide | > 1440px | Max-width container at 1440px; four-column product grid; extended hero with parallax; larger typography (display-xl scales to 56px); additional whitespace around sections |

### Touch Targets
- All buttons and interactive elements maintain a minimum 44px height for touch accessibility
- Nav links have 48px tap targets on mobile
- Quantity selector buttons are 40px tall with 32px tap areas
- Color swatches are 44px on mobile for easier selection
- Accordion triggers have 48px touch targets

### Collapsing Strategy
- Primary nav collapses to a hamburger menu at < 744px, with a slide-out drawer
- Product filters collapse to a "Filter" button with a modal overlay on mobile
- Footer sections collapse to accordion panels on mobile, expanding on tap
- Multi-column layouts collapse to single column on mobile
- Hero sections reduce padding and stack content vertically on mobile
- Search bar collapses to an icon on mobile, expanding to full-width on tap
