## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The primary conversion driver across the site, used for "Get a Quote," "Find a Dealer," and product-page CTAs. Solid #ef4034 red with white uppercase text in Gotham Bold at 14px with 1px letter spacing. Corners are tight at `{rounded.xs}` (4px) — no pill shapes, no playful curves, just the geometric precision of an engineered housing. On hover, the background shifts to #e53935 for a subtle temperature drop; the disabled state desaturates to a muted pink (#f5a8a3) that recedes without disappearing entirely.

**`button-secondary`** — A white-fill button with ink-colored text and a 1px hairline border, deployed for secondary actions like "View Specifications," "Download PDF," and "Compare Models." On hover, the background shifts to the surface-soft tone (#eef0f3) and the border darkens to muted-soft (#8c9091). Same 48px height and `{rounded.xs}` corners as primary for strict visual alignment across button groups.

**`button-dark`** — A dark-background variant using the nav-dark (#1e242b) fill with white text. Appears in hero sections and dark-themed content zones where the primary red would compete with the background imagery. Provides a high-contrast, authoritative action target without the urgency of red.

**`button-ghost`** — A borderless, transparent button with primary-red text. Used for tertiary actions: "Cancel," "Clear Filters," "See All Products." The uppercase Gotham Bold treatment ensures it still reads as an interactive element despite the absence of a visible container.

### Cards
**`product-card`** — The core content unit for heater listings. White surface with `{rounded.sm}` (8px) corners, no outer padding — the image fills the top half at a 4:3 aspect ratio with rounded top corners only. Content sits below with 16px horizontal padding and 24px bottom padding. Product names use Gotham Medium at 16px/500 ({typography.title-sm}), while prices appear in body-md weight 700 for numeric emphasis. A badge overlay (see `product-card-badge`) anchors to the top-left corner for flags like "New" or "Best Seller," rendered in the primary red with white uppercase text.

### Navigation
**`nav-bar`** — A fixed 72px header in deep slate (#1e242b) that creates a dark architectural frame for every page. Navigation links use Gotham Bold at 13px, uppercase with 0.8px letter spacing — the kind of typographic density you find on engineering schematics. Active links are full white; inactive links recede to muted-soft (#8c9091). A small red CTA pill sits in the right zone for "Get a Quote," providing persistent access to the conversion funnel. On scroll, the nav condenses to 64px and the background shifts to the slightly lighter nav-slate (#303742) for depth separation.

**`nav-cta`** — The persistent quote-request button embedded in the navigation bar. Compact at 36px tall with `{rounded.xs}` corners and Gotham Bold button-sm typography. Uses the primary red to pop against the dark navigation background, ensuring the highest-value conversion action is always one tap away.

### Forms
**`text-input`** — Standard input fields with a white background, 1px hairline border, `{rounded.xs}` corners, and 48px height for comfortable touch interaction. On focus, the border thickens to 2px and turns primary red (#ef4034), replacing the typical blue focus ring with brand-consistent color. Error states also use a red border — the brand trusts contextual error messages below the field rather than color-coding the distinction between focus and error.

**`quote-form`** — A contained form panel with the surface-soft (#eef0f3) background, `{rounded.sm}` corners, and `{spacing.xl}` (32px) internal padding. Used for dealer-locator forms, quote requests, and contact panels. The slightly tinted background separates the form from the white canvas without introducing a hard border.

### Hero
**`hero-banner`** — Full-width hero section in deep slate (#1e242b) with large product photography as a background image. The display-xl Bodoni headline sits at 52px — its serif high-contrast strokes evoke the premium hospitality market. An overline in teal-soft (#afcfda) using the uppercase overline typography provides a category label ("Residential Heating" / "Commercial Solutions") above the headline. Two CTAs sit side by side: a solid red primary and a ghost-bordered secondary, both at 52px height for emphatic presence.

### Specification Tables
**`spec-table`** — A structured data display for heater specifications: wattage, voltage, BTU output, dimensions, clearances. The header row uses the nav-dark background with white uppercase spec-label typography (Gotham Bold 11px, 0.8px tracking). Data rows alternate between white and surface-soft (#eef0f3) for scanability. Rows are separated by soft hairline borders. The table uses `{rounded.sm}` on the outer container with overflow hidden to clip the corners.

### Selectors
**`wattage-selector`** — A row of selectable option tiles for choosing heater wattage (1,500W / 2,000W / 3,000W / 4,000W). Inactive tiles have a white background with a hairline border; the active tile fills with primary red and switches to white text. Each tile uses Gotham Medium at 16px ({typography.title-sm}). The selector occupies a key position on product pages, sitting between the product gallery and the add-to-quote button.

**`finish-swatch`** — Circular swatches (32px diameter) for selecting heater finish options: brushed stainless, matte black, bronze, white. Inactive swatches have a 2px hairline border; the active swatch switches to a 2px primary-red border. The swatch fill color is set dynamically to match the actual finish color.

### Gallery
**`gallery-thumbnail`** — Small 72x72px image previews below the main product image. Inactive thumbnails have a 1px hairline border and surface-soft background; the active thumbnail switches to a 2px primary-red border. Thumbnails use `{rounded.xs}` corners to match the system's angular geometry.

### Badges
**`badge-energy`** — A teal (#3d7e94) badge with white text for energy-efficiency ratings and environmental certifications. The color separates it from promotional badges, signaling informational rather than commercial intent.

**`badge-warranty`** — A gold (#fcc141) badge with ink-colored text for warranty duration callouts ("3-Year Warranty," "Lifetime Element Guarantee"). The warm gold tone connects visually to the amber accent family without competing with the primary red.

**`badge-sale`** — A heat-orange (#e85600) badge for promotional pricing and limited-time offers. Hotter and more urgent than the informational teal, but distinct from the primary red so sale badges don't visually merge with CTA buttons.

**`badge-success`** — A green (#32b643) badge for in-stock status, successful form submissions, and confirmation states. Used sparingly — the green appears only when the system needs to communicate positive completion.

### Footer
**`footer`** — A full-width section in nav-dark (#1e242b) with columns for product categories, support links, dealer resources, and company information. Headings use Gotham Medium at 16px in white ({typography.title-sm}), while links appear in muted-soft (#8c9091) and shift to full white on hover. The footer includes a newsletter signup field, social media icons, and legal text in caption-sm.

### Lifestyle Section
**`lifestyle-section`** — A warm-toned content band using the warm-cream background (#eae4d9) to break up the cool gray-and-white rhythm of the main canvas. Used for "Why Infrared" educational content, installation showcase galleries, and testimonial strips. Body text runs at body-lg (18px) for a more editorial reading pace. The warm background connects the digital experience to the physical sensation of radiant heat.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grid; nav collapses to hamburger with slide-in dark drawer; hero headline drops to display-md (28px Gotham Bold); hero CTAs stack vertically at full width; spec tables scroll horizontally; wattage selector wraps to 2x2 grid; gallery thumbnails become a horizontal scroll strip; footer stacks into accordion sections |
| Tablet | 744-1128px | Two-column product grid; nav links visible but condensed with dropdown menus; hero uses display-lg Bodoni (40px); spec tables fit without scroll; wattage selector displays inline; footer arranges in two rows of two columns |
| Desktop | 1128-1440px | Three-column product grid; full nav with mega-menu dropdowns for product categories; hero at full display-xl (52px Bodoni); sticky nav with scroll-state transition; multi-column footer; quote form appears as sidebar panel on product pages |
| Wide | > 1440px | Max-width container at 1440px centered; four-column product grid on category pages; expanded hero imagery with wider aspect ratios; increased section spacing; gallery thumbnail grid expands to show six thumbnails |

### Touch Targets
- All buttons maintain 48px minimum height; the nav-cta at 36px compensates with generous horizontal padding for a 36x100px+ hit area
- Product cards use the full card surface as a tap target, not just the title text
- Navigation hamburger icon is 48x48px for reliable thumb access
- Wattage selector tiles maintain 48px minimum height with 12px internal padding
- Finish swatches at 32px diameter are spaced 12px apart, meeting the 44px effective target with padding
- Gallery thumbnails at 72x72px exceed minimum touch requirements
- Form inputs hold 48px height across all breakpoints

### Collapsing Strategy
- Top navigation collapses to a hamburger menu below 744px, opening a full-height dark (#1e242b) drawer with stacked nav links
- Product grid collapses from 4 columns (wide) to 3 (desktop) to 2 (tablet) to 1 (mobile)
- Spec tables switch from fixed layout to horizontally scrollable on mobile, with the first column (spec name) pinned
- Hero dual-CTA layout stacks vertically on mobile with both buttons expanding to full width
- Footer columns collapse into an accordion pattern on mobile, with section headings as tappable toggles
- Quote form moves from a sidebar panel to a full-width section below the product gallery on mobile
- Gallery thumbnails switch from a grid to a horizontal scroll strip on mobile
- Wattage selector wraps from a single row to a 2x2 grid on mobile
