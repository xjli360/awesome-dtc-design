## Components

### Buttons

**`button-primary`** — Solid hunter green (#2c4a3e) rectangle with white uppercase Inter text at 14px, letter-spaced at 0.8px. Hover shifts to a deeper #1e3a2e with a 180ms ease-out transition. Disabled state lightens to a desaturated sage (#7a9a8e) at reduced opacity. The uppercase tracking and tight `{rounded.xs}` corner radius give CTAs a catalog-plate formality that suits the brand's heritage positioning.

**`button-secondary`** — White fill with a 1px green border and green uppercase text. On hover the fill inverts to solid green with white type, creating a clean two-state flip. Used for secondary actions like "Request a Quote," "Find a Dealer," and alternate CTAs paired beside a primary button.

**`button-tertiary`** — No background, no border, underlined green text at `{typography.button-sm}`. Used for inline editorial links such as "View Full Collection" at the bottom of product grids or "Learn More" within material description blocks.

### Navigation

**`nav-bar`** — A 72px white bar with a hairline bottom border. The Kingsley Bate wordmark sits left, category links (Dining, Seating, Lounging, Accessories, Collections) span the center in Inter Medium 14px, and utility icons (search, dealer locator) sit right. On scroll, the bottom border drops away and a subtle box-shadow appears. The nav is slightly taller than typical e-commerce headers, giving the brand logo room to breathe.

**`nav-dropdown`** — Full-width mega-menu that opens on hover with no border-radius. Organized in columns: product categories on the left, featured collections in the center with thumbnail imagery, and a large lifestyle photograph on the right edge. Background is white with an 8px-blur drop shadow. Links use `{typography.nav-link-sm}` and darken to `{colors.ink}` on hover.

### Product Display

**`product-card`** — Landscape image (4:3 aspect ratio) with no border-radius, followed by collection name in `{typography.caption}` muted text, product title in `{typography.title-sm}`, and price in `{typography.price}`. No explicit card border or background — the canvas color creates separation. Hover applies a subtle 2px upward translate on the image with a light shadow. The landscape ratio suits outdoor furniture where width matters more than height.

**`material-swatch`** — 36px circles for selecting teak finish, wicker color, or aluminum powder-coat. Unselected swatches show a transparent 2px border; selected ones gain a 2px green border with a 2px inner gap. Label text in `{typography.caption}` appears below on PDP. Kingsley Bate offers six wicker colors against four aluminum finishes, making the swatch row a critical selection surface.

**`finish-selector`** — Rectangular pills for choosing materials (Teak, Wicker, Stainless Steel, Aluminum). Neutral background with a hairline border; selected state swaps to a green 1px border. Sits above the swatch row on PDP to establish the material before the color is chosen.

**`badge-new`** — Small brass-gold (#b09a6a) label with white text, positioned absolute top-left of the product card image. The warm tone echoes oiled teak and avoids the harshness of a red sale badge on a nature-forward brand.

**`badge-bestseller`** — Hunter green (#2c4a3e) with white text. Same size and position as badge-new. Used to highlight perennial lines like the Nantucket and Chelsea collections.

### Hero & Marketing

**`hero-banner`** — Full-bleed lifestyle photograph of a furnished outdoor setting — harbor views, garden walls, poolside terraces — with centered serif display text at `{typography.display-xl}` overlaid on a dark scrim (25% black). CTA button sits below the headline. On mobile, the image crops to a top section while text moves below on the warm canvas. Minimum height 600px on desktop.

**`collection-gateway`** — Grid of landscape images (3:2) linking to top-level collections. Each gateway tile carries a centered uppercase label in `{typography.collection-label}` over a subtle dark overlay. Gateway grids typically show 3 tiles per row on desktop, 2 on tablet, and 1 full-width on mobile. No card border or radius — the photographic edge is the boundary.

**`promo-bar`** — Persistent 40px strip above the nav. Solid green background with white caption text announcing trade shows, new collection launches, or dealer events. Dismiss button right-aligned.

### Commerce & Utility

**`dealer-locator-card`** — Card with soft border and minimal shadow listing dealer name, address, phone, and distance. Used in the "Where to Buy" map view. Green text for the dealer name links to external sites.

**`image-gallery`** — Product detail page gallery with a large main image (no border-radius) and a row of 72px square thumbnails below. Selected thumbnail gains a 2px green border. Thumbnails scroll horizontally when the count exceeds the visible row. Supports pinch-zoom on mobile.

**`image-carousel`** — Homepage and collection page carousel (powered by Slick) with 40px circular arrow buttons, white background with a hairline border. Pagination dots below: 8px circles, hairline gray inactive, green active. Used for "Featured Collections" and "New Arrivals" sections.

### Search

**`search-overlay`** — Centered dropdown from the nav search icon. Contains a full-width text input on a neutral surface (#edebe5) with a left-aligned magnifier. Below: suggested collections and popular product thumbnails in a two-column grid. Overlay sits on a white card with a large box-shadow.

### Footer

**`footer`** — Full-width hunter green (#2c4a3e) background with white and semi-transparent white text. Four columns: Collections, Materials, About (heritage story, sustainability, trade program), and Contact (dealer locator, FAQ, trade show schedule). Headings use `{typography.overline}` — 11px uppercase with 1.5px letter-spacing. Links at 70% white opacity lighten to full white on hover. A secondary row below carries copyright, privacy policy, and social icons.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grid; hamburger replaces nav links; hero text stacks below image on canvas; collection gateways go full-width single-column; footer collapses to accordion; carousel arrows hidden in favor of swipe; material swatches enlarge to 44px for touch |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; nav links visible but abbreviated (icons replace some labels); hero image height reduces to 440px; collection gateway shows 2-up grid; mega-menu becomes scrollable single column |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three-column product grid at `{spacing.xl}` gutters; full mega-menu with lifestyle imagery; hero at 600px min-height; collection gateway at 3-up; footer fully expanded in four columns |
| Wide | > 1440px | Content max-width caps at 1440px and centers on canvas; product grid holds three columns with wider gutters; hero imagery extends full-bleed while text container stays centered at max 1200px; footer content centers within 1200px |

### Touch Targets

- All interactive elements maintain a minimum 44×44px tap area on mobile, even when visually smaller (icon buttons use padding to expand the hit zone)
- Material swatches enlarge from 36px to 44px on touch devices with increased gap (12px vs 8px)
- Nav hamburger icon sits within a 48px tap target
- Carousel arrows enlarge to 48px on tablet; hidden entirely on mobile in favor of swipe gestures
- Finish selector pills maintain 44px minimum height on mobile with increased horizontal padding

### Collapsing Strategy

- Navigation collapses to a slide-out drawer from the left with full-height overlay and accordion-style category groups; collection thumbnails are retained in the mobile drawer
- Product filters collapse to a bottom-sheet modal on mobile with a sticky "Apply" button at the bottom
- Footer columns collapse to expandable accordion sections with plus/minus toggle icons
- Mega-menu imagery is hidden on mobile; only text links remain in the nav drawer
- Breadcrumbs truncate to show only the parent and current page on mobile, with a left-arrow replacing the full path
- Image gallery thumbnails move from a horizontal strip to a scrollable row below the main image on mobile
