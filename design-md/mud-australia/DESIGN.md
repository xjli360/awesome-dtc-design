description: Mud Australia is a celebration of handmade Australian porcelain, where the raw beauty of clay meets a restrained, earthy palette. The brand's soul lives in the quiet tension between the deep, almost charcoal ink of `#101820` and the warm, sun-bleached canvas of `#f8f6f1`. Signature design moves include the use of a single, muted accent—a soft blush `#e7b5aa` or a muted sage `#243127`—that appears like a natural patina on a piece of pottery. The typography, set in the clean, humanist `NeueHaasUnicaPro-Light`, feels light and airy, never heavy, allowing the form and texture of the porcelain to take center stage. The overall mood is one of serene, grounded sophistication. It feels like a curated collection of heirlooms, where each piece has a story told through its subtle variations in glaze and form. The palette of `#e4e0d9`, `#e7e7e7`, and `#f2f5f5` creates a soft, diffused light, while touches of `#63666a` and `#b4b4b4` ground the design with a sense of quiet permanence. This is a brand that trusts the material, the maker's hand, and the power of negative space.

## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The primary call-to-action, rendered in the brand's deep ink `{colors.primary}` with light text `{colors.on-primary}`. It has no rounded corners, emphasizing a clean, architectural feel. On hover, it transitions to `{colors.primary-active}`. The disabled state uses `{colors.primary-disabled}`. The button uses an uppercase, letter-spaced typography (`{typography.button-md}`) for a deliberate, editorial tone.

**`button-secondary`** — A transparent button with a single-pixel hairline border `{colors.hairline}` and ink text. It is used for less prominent actions, such as "View Details" or "Cancel". On hover, the background fills with `{colors.surface-soft}` and the border becomes solid `{colors.ink}`.

**`button-tertiary-text`** — A plain text button with no background or border. It is used for inline actions like "Clear" or "Apply" within filters or search. It inherits the `{typography.button-md}` style but has no padding or container.

### Cards
**`product-card`** — A minimal, borderless card on the `{colors.canvas}` background. It contains an image area (`product-card-image`) with a soft `{colors.surface-soft}` placeholder, a title using `{typography.title-sm}`, and a price in `{colors.muted}`. On hover, a subtle shadow (`boxShadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(16, 24, 32, 0.08)`) lifts the card. There are no rounded corners, maintaining the brand's clean, unadorned aesthetic.

### Navigation
**`nav-bar`** — A fixed top navigation bar at 72px height on a `{colors.canvas}` background. Navigation links use `{typography.nav-link}` with uppercase styling and 0.5px letter spacing. The active link is underlined with a 1px solid `{colors.ink}` border. On scroll, a soft bottom border (`{colors.hairline-soft}`) appears.

### Forms
**`text-input`** — A simple, border-defined input field with no rounded corners. It uses `{typography.body-md}` for legibility. On focus, the border switches from `{colors.hairline}` to `{colors.ink}`. An error state uses `{colors.accent-terracotta}` for the border.

### Badges
**`collection-badge`**, **`sale-badge`**, **`new-badge`** — Small, uppercase badges used to denote product attributes. They have no rounded corners and use `{typography.badge}`. The collection badge is sage green (`{colors.accent-sage}`), the sale badge is terracotta (`{colors.accent-terracotta}`), and the new badge is blush (`{colors.accent-blush}`).

### Footer
**`footer-section`** — A full-width footer with a deep `{colors.primary}` background and light text. Links use `{typography.link}` and transition to `{colors.muted-soft}` on hover. The footer is a key area for the brand's earthy, grounded feel.

### Search
**`search-bar`** — An input field with a `{colors.surface-soft}` background and a soft hairline border. It is designed to blend into the page, not stand out. On focus, the border becomes `{colors.ink}`.

### Accordion
**`accordion-trigger`** — A text-only trigger with a bottom border. It uses `{typography.title-sm}` and expands to reveal `accordion-content` below. This pattern is used for product details and FAQs.

### Quantity Selector
**`quantity-selector`** — A compact, bordered input for selecting product quantities. It has no rounded corners and uses `{typography.body-md}`.

### Add to Cart Button
**`add-to-cart-button`** — A larger, more prominent version of the primary button (56px height) used specifically on product detail pages.

### Color Swatch
**`color-swatch`** — A circular (full rounded) swatch for selecting product colors. The selected state is indicated by a 2px solid `{colors.ink}` border.

### Breadcrumb
**`breadcrumb-link`** and **`breadcrumb-current`** — Small, muted links for navigation. The current page is rendered in `{colors.ink}`.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grids, stacked navigation, full-width hero, accordion-style footer, reduced padding/section spacing to 48px. |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grids, horizontal navigation, side-by-side hero content, standard section spacing (80px). |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three-column product grids, full navigation with dropdowns, multi-column footer, maximum content width of 1128px. |
| Wide | > 1440px | Four-column product grids, expanded hero, maximum content width of 1440px with generous margins. |

### Touch Targets
- All interactive elements (buttons, links, inputs) have a minimum height of 44px.
- Color swatches are 44px x 44px on mobile for easy tapping.
- Navigation links have a minimum touch area of 44px x 44px.
- Accordion triggers have a minimum height of 48px.

### Collapsing Strategy
- On mobile, the top navigation collapses into a hamburger menu.
- The footer's multi-column layout collapses into a single-column accordion.
- Product filters collapse into a slide-out drawer.
- The hero section's text and image stack vertically.
