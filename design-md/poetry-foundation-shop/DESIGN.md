## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The primary call-to-action, rendered in the brand's signature red (#ed1c24) with white text. Uses Gill Sans at 15px weight 600 with 0.3px letter spacing for a confident, literary feel. Corners are gently squared at {rounded.sm} (8px). On hover, the background deepens to {colors.primary-active} (#a31418). Disabled state fades to {colors.primary-disabled} (#e5422b) with reduced opacity.

**`button-secondary`** — An outlined or ghost variant on the warm canvas background (#f1efea). Text is the deep ink (#2b2b2b). On hover, the background fills with the soft hairline (#d5d6d2). Used for "Add to Wishlist" and secondary navigation actions.

**`button-tertiary-text`** — A text-only button with no background, using the primary red for its text. Appears in compact contexts like "Read More" links or "Remove" actions. Hover state adds an underline.

**`button-accent-blue`** — A secondary accent button using the brand's blue (#00b2ff). Appears in contexts where red would be too aggressive — "Learn More" about a poetry collection, or "Subscribe to Newsletter." Active state shifts to #0096db.

**`button-pill-primary`** — A smaller, fully rounded variant of the primary button ({rounded.full}). Used for filter tags, category pills, and compact CTAs in card layouts. Height is 36px with 8px vertical padding.

**`button-pill-secondary`** — The pill-shaped counterpart on canvas background. Used for "All Poems," "New Releases," and other filter toggles. Active state fills with the primary red.

### Cards
**`product-card`** — A white card ({colors.surface-card}) with {rounded.sm} corners containing a product image, title, author, price, and optional badges. On hover, the entire card lifts with a subtle shadow and the background shifts to {colors.surface-soft} (#f1f1f1). The image area occupies the top 60% of the card and uses {rounded.sm} for its top corners.

**`product-card-image`** — The image container within a product card. Uses a soft gray background (#f1f1f1) as placeholder while images load. Aspect ratio is 3:4 for book covers, 1:1 for merchandise.

**`product-badge`** — A small, uppercase label pinned to the top-left of product card images. Red background (#ed1c24) with white text in 11px weight 700. Corners are {rounded.xs} (4px). Used for "Staff Pick," "Signed Edition," "Limited."

**`product-badge-sale`** — A yellow variant (#ffee58) with dark text for sale and discount indicators. Same dimensions and typography as the standard badge.

**`product-badge-new`** — A blue variant (#00b2ff) for "New Release" and "Just Added" labels. Same dimensions and typography.

### Navigation
**`nav-bar`** — A fixed top navigation bar at 64px height on the warm canvas background. Navigation links use Gill Sans at 15px weight 600 with 0.5px letter spacing, all uppercase — a deliberate choice that reads as editorial rather than commercial. The Poetry Foundation logo sits on the left in Baskerville.

**`nav-link-active`** — The active navigation state, where the link text shifts to the primary red (#ed1c24). No underline or background fill — the color change alone signals the current section.

**`nav-dropdown`** — A dropdown panel that appears on hover over nav items like "Shop," "Poems," "Learn." White background with {rounded.sm} corners, containing links in body-md weight 400. Dividers between sections use {colors.hairline}.

### Forms
**`text-input`** — A standard text input field on the warm canvas background. Height is 44px with 10px vertical padding and 16px horizontal. Typography is body-md (Gill Sans 16px). On focus, a 2px border in the primary red appears. Error state uses {colors.error} (#a31418) for the border.

**`search-bar`** — A dedicated search input styled identically to text-input but with a search icon on the left. On focus, the border shifts to primary red. Placeholder text reads "Search poems, poets, books..." in {colors.muted} (#767676).

### Footer
**`footer`** — A dark footer section with {colors.ink} (#2b2b2b) background and white text. Contains three columns: About, Shop, and Connect. Links are body-sm weight 400 with {spacing.sm} between items. The Poetry Foundation copyright line sits at the bottom in {colors.muted-soft} (#aeaeae).

**`footer-link`** — White text links in the footer. On hover, the link color shifts to the primary red (#ed1c24), creating a consistent interaction pattern across the site.

### Content Blocks
**`hero-section`** — A full-width hero area on the warm canvas background. Uses display-xl in Baskerville (36px weight 700) for the headline, with body-lg below. Padding is {spacing.section} (64px) vertically and {spacing.lg} (24px) horizontally.

**`hero-section-accent`** — A red-background hero variant using {colors.primary}. White text. Used for featured collections, seasonal promotions, and special announcements.

**`quote-block`** — A highlighted quote or excerpt from a poem, set on a soft gray background (#f1f1f1) with {rounded.sm} corners. Uses the italic Baskerville quote style at 22px. Padding is {spacing.lg} (24px) on all sides.

**`poem-block`** — The primary text container for poem content. Uses Baskerville at 16px with generous line-height (1.7) for readability. No background — the poem sits directly on the canvas. Line breaks and stanza spacing are preserved.

**`divider`** — A 1px horizontal rule in {colors.hairline} (#c4c8d8). Used between major sections. The soft variant uses {colors.hairline-soft} (#d5d6d2) for less visual weight within content areas.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column layout; nav collapses to hamburger; product cards stack vertically at full width; hero text reduces to display-md (24px); footer stacks to single column; search bar moves to persistent bottom bar |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; nav links remain visible but condensed; hero uses display-lg (28px); sidebar content moves below main content; footer shows two columns |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three-column product grid; full nav with dropdowns; hero at full display-xl; sidebar visible on left; footer shows three columns |
| Wide | > 1440px | Max-width container at 1280px centered; four-column product grid; hero text scales to 40px; additional whitespace on sides; footer expands to four columns |

### Touch Targets
- All interactive elements (buttons, links, inputs) maintain minimum 44px height for touch accessibility
- Icon buttons are 40px × 40px minimum with 36px circle variants
- Product cards have full-surface tap targets (no dead zones)
- Nav dropdowns appear on tap with 300ms delay to prevent accidental triggers
- Search bar expands to full width on mobile with 48px height for thumb reachability

### Collapsing Strategy
- Top navigation collapses to a hamburger menu on mobile (< 744px), revealing a full-screen overlay with larger touch targets
- Product grid collapses from 4 columns → 3 → 2 → 1 as viewport shrinks
- Hero section reduces vertical padding from 64px to 32px on mobile
- Footer columns collapse from 4 → 3 → 2 → 1
- Sidebar content (categories, filters) moves to a bottom sheet on mobile, triggered by a "Filter" button
- Quote blocks reduce padding from 24px to 16px on mobile
- Badge text may truncate or stack on very narrow screens (< 400px)
