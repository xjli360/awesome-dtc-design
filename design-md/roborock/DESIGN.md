## Components

### Buttons

**`button-primary`** — Cobalt blue (#4b71d9) fill with white label at 16px/600 weight, 48px tall with {rounded.sm} corners and 24px horizontal padding. Hover darkens to #3a5cb8 (`button-primary-active`); disabled washes to #a8bcee with unchanged geometry. This is the sole high-intent CTA across PDPs, cart, and checkout.

**`button-secondary`** — White canvas with a 1px `{colors.primary}` border and matching blue label; mirrors primary dimensions exactly so CTA pairs align without optical size mismatch. On dark hero modules, `button-secondary-dark` swaps to a transparent background with white border and white text against `{colors.dark-stage}`.

**`button-ghost`** — Transparent background, primary blue label, no border; used for inline actions such as "Learn more" within product cards, feature callouts, and comparison rows where a full button weight would crowd the layout.

### Text Input

**`text-input`** — 44px height, 8px radius, 1px `{colors.hairline}` border on idle. Placeholder text renders in `{colors.muted}`. Focus shifts the border to `{colors.primary}` with no additional shadow — the single border-color change is sufficient at this font size. Used across search, email capture, account forms, and checkout address fields.

### Navigation Bar

**`nav-bar`** — 64px tall, white canvas, 1px hairline-soft bottom border. Logo occupies a reserved 140px zone at left; main category links spread center in `{typography.nav-link}` (14px/500); account, wishlist, and cart icons sit at right. On scroll the bar picks up a light drop shadow without a background change. Mobile collapses to hamburger + logo + cart — no category labels visible until the drawer is open.

### Product Card

**`product-card`** — Soft-white (#f8f8f8) background, 1px hairline-soft perimeter border, 12px radius. Product image renders on a `{colors.surface-soft}` swatch zone that prevents color-cast artifacts from white-background product photography. Title in `{typography.title-sm}`, price in `{typography.price-display}` cobalt, descriptive copy in `{typography.body-sm}` muted gray. `badge-sale` or `badge-new` pins to the top-left corner of the image zone. Entire card surface is tappable on mobile.

### Hero Modules

**`hero-dark`** — Full-bleed dark-stage (#161616) canvas, minimum 600px height. White display title in `{typography.display-xl}` (48px/700), subtitle in `{typography.body-md}`. Product renders center-right as a transparent-background cutout — no drop shadow, no halo — so the dark field reads as the product's natural environment. Primary CTA (`button-primary`) and secondary (`button-secondary-dark`) sit left-aligned below the headline at 24px gap.

**`hero-split`** — White canvas, 50/50 column split: text block left, product image right. Title in `{typography.display-md}` ink, subtitle in `{typography.body-md}`. Used for mid-page feature announcements and accessory callouts where product detail legibility outweighs drama.

### Spec Table

**`spec-table`** — Surface-soft (#f6f6f6) base with a `{colors.dark-stage}` header row carrying column titles in white `{typography.spec-label}` weight. Alternating row fills (surface-soft / white) aid line-by-line scansability. Spec labels in 13px/500, values in `{typography.body-sm}`. Rounded at {rounded.md}, 1px hairline border perimeter. On mobile the table scrolls horizontally with the label column sticky at left.

### Badges

**`badge-sale`** — Accent red (#ea0029), uppercase 11px/700, 4px radius, 3×8px padding. Appears on product cards and adjacent to struck-through original prices. **`badge-new`** — Same geometry in primary blue (#4b71d9); signals recent product launches. **`badge-bestseller`** — Dark-stage black background, same geometry; anchors authority claims without urgency color coding.

### Category Tabs

**`category-tab`** — Borderless horizontal tab row; inactive links in `{colors.muted}` 16px/600, active link in `{colors.ink}` with a 2px `{colors.primary}` underline. No background fill on any state — only text color and underline change. Padding 12px top and bottom. On mobile the tab strip becomes a horizontal scroll container with no clipping indicator.

### Feature Callout

**`feature-callout`** — Surface-soft tile, 12px radius, 24px padding. Primary-blue icon above a `{typography.title-sm}` heading and `{typography.body-sm}` body copy. Arranged in a 3–4 column grid on desktop, used in PDPs to itemize LiDAR precision, suction ratings, auto-empty docking, and mapping technology. The icon treatment is monoline at approximately 24px; icon library details are unconfirmed (see Known Gaps).

### Status Indicator

**`status-indicator`** — 8px filled dot (status-green #68d44f for active/charged states; accent-red #ea0029 for errors/alerts) followed by `{typography.caption}` label text. Appears inside cleaning-map UI illustrations and app-screenshot embeds on product detail pages; not used as a standalone web UI element outside of embedded app mockups.

### Promo Banner

**`promo-banner`** — Full-bleed dark-stage background, white display title in `{typography.display-md}`, promo-red (#f92c50) accent on countdown text or percentage-off callouts. CTA in `button-primary` or `button-secondary-dark`. 48px vertical padding top and bottom. Deployed at page top for sitewide sale events and above-the-fold campaign modules.

### Search Bar

**`search-bar`** — Pill-shaped ({rounded.full}), 44px height, surface-soft fill with hairline border and muted-gray search icon at left. Focus shifts border to `{colors.primary}`; the pill does not resize or relocate — it expands in place. Used in the desktop nav header and as a dedicated mobile search entry point at the top of category pages.

### Comparison Card

**`comparison-card`** — White canvas, 12px radius. The recommended model variant gets a 2px `{colors.primary}` border; all others use 1px hairline. Title in `{typography.title-md}`, price in `{typography.price-display}`, spec rows in `{typography.spec-label}`. 32px internal padding. Arranged 2–4 across on desktop for robot vacuum model comparison pages; collapses to a snap-scroll horizontal carousel on mobile.

### Footer

**`footer`** — Dark-stage (#161616) background, white primary text, `{colors.border-mid}` (#babfc3) secondary links. Column headings in `{typography.title-sm}` white/600, links in `{typography.body-sm}` border-mid. Social icons inline in the bottom link bar. 64px vertical padding. Certifications and legal copy render in `{typography.caption}` muted-soft at the very bottom.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grid; nav collapses to hamburger + logo + cart; hero-dark portrait-crops to 480px min-height with text stacked above product; spec-table scrolls horizontally with sticky label column; category tabs become horizontal scroll strip; comparison cards become snap-scroll carousel |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | 2-column product grid; nav shows top-level labels without mega-menu; hero-split stacks to image-over-text; feature-callout 2 per row; comparison cards 2-up |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | 3–4 column product grid; full nav with mega-menu dropdowns; hero-dark full 600px height; spec-table full width; comparison cards 4-up |
| Wide | > 1440px | Content max-width 1440px centered; hero horizontal padding expands to expose more product image; product grid holds at 4 columns with increased card gap; generous lateral margins on editorial sections |

### Touch Targets

- All interactive elements minimum 44×44px on mobile
- Category tab strip expands to 48px touch height despite smaller visual text treatment
- Nav icons (cart, account, search) padded to 44px tap target with transparent hit area
- Product card entire surface is tappable on mobile — no separate image vs. title tap zones
- Badge elements are non-interactive; no minimum size constraint applies

### Collapsing Strategy

- Mega-menu nav collapses to a full-screen drawer on mobile; top-level categories become an accordion with chevron toggles
- Spec table wraps in a horizontal-scroll container; the first (label) column is sticky so spec names stay visible while values scroll
- Comparison cards collapse from 4-up grid to a single-card snap-scroll carousel with dot pagination
- Feature callout grid reflows 4-col → 3-col → 2-col → 1-col across breakpoints
- Promo banner maintains full-bleed at all widths; headline and CTA stack vertically on mobile with centered alignment
- Hero-dark drops from side-by-side to stacked (text above, product below) on mobile; product image crops to square aspect ratio

## Known Gaps

- No proprietary icon library identified; Roborock likely ships a custom glyph set not surfaced in CSS extraction — icon style (filled vs. outline, stroke weight, corner treatment) is inferred from engineering-brand context and cannot be confirmed
- Exact mega-menu dropdown background treatment (white canvas vs. surface-soft) could not be confirmed from static extraction
- Animation and transition timing (hover durations, parallax scroll on hero modules, drawer slide speed) are not recoverable from a static scrape
- In-app cleaning-map palette (room-zone fill colors, path trace colors, obstacle markers) lives in native app assets, not web tokens — only the status-green and accent-red are confirmed web-surfaced
- Proxima-nova weight axis loaded on site (whether a full variable font or discrete 400/600/700 instances) is unconfirmed; only those three weights are used above
- Dark-mode support undetermined — no `prefers-color-scheme` media query was detected in CSS extraction
- Shopify theme section padding overrides and custom section spacing may deviate from the spacing scale defined here; exact rhythm between page sections requires live inspection
