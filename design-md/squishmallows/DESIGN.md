## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The primary call-to-action across the site, rendered in #2d27ff electric blue with white text and a soft 8px radius. On hover, shifts to #1d52e1 deeper blue; disabled state uses #a89cc8 lavender-gray. Used for “Add to Cart,” “Shop Now,” and “View All” actions. **`button-secondary`** — An outlined variant with a white fill, #2d27ff text, and a 2px solid border. Used for “Learn More” and “See Details” links alongside primary buttons. **`button-pill-primary`** — A fully rounded pill version of the primary button, used for filter tags and “Shop by Squad” navigation items. **`button-pill-accent`** — Same pill shape but in #f83568 hot pink, used for limited-time promotions and “New Arrivals” quick links. **`button-cart`** — A teal (#108474) button reserved for cart-related actions like “Checkout” and “Update Cart,” providing visual distinction from the primary blue.

### Navigation
**`top-nav`** — A sticky 72px white bar with a #1a1a1a logo, centered category links, and a right-aligned cart icon. Category links use `nav-link-inactive` (#7b7b7b muted) by default and `nav-link-active` (#2d27ff primary with a 2px bottom border) for the current section. The nav collapses to a hamburger menu on mobile. **`search-bar-pill`** — A fully rounded search field with #d4f3fa light cyan background, placeholder text in #7b7b7b, and a magnifying glass icon. Expands to full width on mobile.

### Cards
**`product-card`** — A white card with a 20px radius and subtle shadow (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)). The photo area occupies the top with a 1:1 aspect ratio and rounded top corners. Product name uses `title-sm` (16px, 600 weight), price uses `price` (18px, 700 weight), and sale prices render in #ed1d3d crimson. On hover, the shadow deepens to 0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.12). **`product-card-badge`** — Positioned absolutely at the top-left of the card. “New” badges use #ebe717 marigold with dark text, “Sale” badges use #ed1d3d crimson with white text, and “Limited Edition” badges use #f83568 hot pink with white text. All badges use 11px uppercase bold type with 0.5px letter spacing.

### Hero & Banners
**`hero-banner`** — A full-width 400px section with #f9fafb soft background, large display type (36–48px), and a single `hero-cta` button. The CTA uses `button-lg` (18px, 700 weight) with 16px/32px padding. Hero images are typically full-bleed product photography with the CTA overlaid. **`category-tile`** — A 20px radius card used for collection navigation (e.g., “Fuzzy Squad,” “Holiday Squad”). Standard tiles use white with shadow; accent tiles use #6e3778 purple with white text for featured categories.

### Forms & Inputs
**`text-input`** — Standard form input with 12px padding, 8px radius, #eeeeee border, and #ffffff background. Focus state uses #2d27ff border. **`newsletter-input`** — Email signup field with 12px padding, 8px radius, and #eeeeee border. **`newsletter-submit`** — A #f83568 hot pink submit button paired with the newsletter input. **`quantity-selector`** — A compact 40px control with #f9fafb background, used on product detail pages for adjusting cart quantities.

### Footer
**`footer`** — A full-width #1a1a1a section with white text. Links use #9ca3af muted-soft color and `link` typography. The footer includes columns for “Shop,” “About,” “Help,” and “Connect,” plus a newsletter signup row and social media icons.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column grid, hamburger nav, full-width search, 2-column product grid, hero height reduces to 250px |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | 3-column product grid, visible nav links (no hamburger), 2-row category tiles, hero at 350px |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | 4-column product grid, full nav with dropdowns, 3-row category tiles, hero at 400px |
| Wide | > 1440px | Max-width 1440px container, 5-column product grid, expanded footer columns |

### Touch Targets
- All buttons and interactive elements maintain minimum 44px height for touch accessibility
- Product card tap targets (add to cart, quick view) are at least 48px
- Nav links have 48px minimum touch area even when text is smaller
- Quantity selector buttons are 40px with adequate spacing
- Search bar is 48px tall for easy tapping

### Collapsing Strategy
- Top nav collapses to hamburger menu below 744px; category dropdowns become accordion sections
- Product grid reduces from 5 columns (wide) to 2 columns (mobile)
- Footer columns stack vertically on mobile, with expandable section headers
- Hero banner reduces height and stacks CTA below text on mobile
- Search bar expands to full width on mobile, hiding the nav search icon
- Category tiles switch from horizontal scroll (desktop) to vertical stack (mobile)

## Known Gaps

- Hover and focus states for most components (beyond primary button) could not be reliably extracted from the live site
- Error styling for form inputs (validation messages, error borders) is not documented
- Dark mode is not supported and no dark-mode color tokens exist
- Sub-brand palettes (e.g., “Fuzzy Squad,” “Holiday Squad”) may have additional accent colors not captured
- The extracted font list includes many fallback/system fonts; the primary brand font appears to be Nunito, but Comfortaa and freight-sans-pro may be used in specific contexts (e.g., headings, promotional banners)
- Shopify checkout widget colors (e.g., #00b6bd, #013b38) may not be brand colors but platform defaults
- Social media icon colors (e.g., #ff0000 for YouTube, #0f99fe for Twitter) are included in the extracted hex list and may not be brand-specific
- Stock image dominant tones may have influenced the extracted color list; the brand’s true secondary palette may be more limited than the 30+ hex values suggest
- Animation and transition durations are not documented
- Loading states, skeleton screens, and empty states are not captured
- Print stylesheet behavior is unknown
- Accessibility contrast ratios have not been verified against the documented color pairs
