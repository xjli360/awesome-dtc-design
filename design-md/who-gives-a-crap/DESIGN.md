## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The brand's signature green pill, used for all primary CTAs: "Shop Now", "Subscribe", "Add to Cart". Full-width on mobile, auto-width on desktop. Hover shifts to `{colors.primary-active}` (#24a87d). Disabled state uses `{colors.primary-disabled}` (#b3f0db) with white text — still readable but clearly inactive.

**`button-secondary`** — An outlined button with a 2px `{colors.ink}` border and white fill. Used for secondary actions like "Learn More" or "View Details". Hover fills with `{colors.surface-soft}` (#f5f5f5). Same pill shape and typography as primary.

**`button-tertiary`** — A text-only button in `{colors.primary}` green, no background or border. Used for inline actions like "See all options" or "Edit subscription". Hover adds a subtle `{colors.surface-soft}` background.

**`button-pill-green`** — A smaller, compact version of the primary pill (40px height vs 48px). Used for filter tags, category pills, and subscription-tier selectors. Same green fill, same full rounding.

**`button-pill-outline`** — A smaller outlined pill (40px height) with a 1px `{colors.hairline}` border. Used for secondary filter options and "Compare" actions. Hover may promote to green border.

### Cards
**`product-card`** — A white card with a 1px `{colors.hairline-soft}` border and `{rounded.md}` (12px) corners. Contains a square product image (`{rounded.sm}`), title in `{typography.title-sm}`, price in `{typography.body-md}`, and optional badges. Hover elevates with a subtle shadow and slightly stronger border. Used for the product grid on the shop page.

**`product-card-badge`** — A small green badge overlaid on the product image corner. Uses `{typography.badge}` (12px, uppercase, bold) with `{colors.badge-green}` background. Variants include `badge-new` (green), `badge-sale` (red), and `badge-eco` (light green with dark green text).

### Navigation
**`nav-bar`** — A 72px white bar with a 1px `{colors.hairline}` bottom border. Contains the brand logo (left), nav links (center), and cart/account icons (right). Nav links use `{typography.nav-link}` (14px, 600 weight). Active and hover states tint the link to `{colors.primary}`. On scroll, a subtle box shadow appears. On mobile (< 744px), the center links collapse into a hamburger menu.

**`mobile-menu`** — A full-width white panel that slides in from the left or top. Uses larger nav link typography (18px, 600 weight) with generous padding. Includes the same link hover/active states as the desktop nav.

### Forms
**`text-input`** — A standard input field with white background, 1px `{colors.hairline}` border, and `{rounded.sm}` (8px) corners. 48px height with 12px/16px padding. Focus state swaps to a 2px `{colors.primary}` border. Error state uses a 2px `{colors.error}` (#d32f2f) border.

**`select-input`** — Same dimensions and styling as `text-input`, used for dropdown selectors (e.g., quantity, subscription frequency).

**`checkbox`** — A 20px square with 2px `{colors.hairline}` border and `{rounded.xs}` (4px) corners. Checked state fills with `{colors.primary}` green. Used for subscription add-ons and terms acceptance.

**`radio`** — A 20px circle with 2px `{colors.hairline}` border and `{rounded.full}`. Checked state shows a green border with a filled green center dot. Used for subscription plan selection.

**`toggle-switch`** — A 48x28 pill with gray background and a white 24px circular knob. Active state fills the pill with `{colors.primary}` green. Used for subscription auto-refill toggles.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Nav links collapse to hamburger; hero padding reduces; product grid goes single-column; buttons become full-width; footer columns stack vertically |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Nav remains expanded; product grid shows 2 columns; hero maintains padding but headline shrinks slightly; footer shows 2-column layout |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Full layout: 3-4 column product grid; nav fully visible; hero at max width; footer in 4 columns |
| Wide | > 1440px | Content max-width constrained to ~1200px; margins increase; hero section may use full-bleed background |

### Touch Targets
- All buttons and interactive elements minimum 44px height (48px standard)
- Nav links have 44px minimum tap area
- Quantity selector buttons are 48px squares
- Checkbox and radio controls are 20px with 44px tap area via padding
- Hamburger icon is 24px with 44px tap area

### Collapsing Strategy
- Desktop nav links (center) collapse into hamburger menu below 744px
- Product grid collapses from 4 columns (desktop) → 2 columns (tablet) → 1 column (mobile)
- Footer collapses from 4 columns (desktop) → 2 columns (tablet) → stacked (mobile)
- Hero section padding reduces from `{spacing.section}` to `{spacing.xxl}` on mobile
- Accordion sections remain collapsed by default on all breakpoints
- Search bar may collapse into icon-only on mobile, expanding on tap
