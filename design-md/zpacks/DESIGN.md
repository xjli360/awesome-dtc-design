description: A backcountry gear brand that paints its identity in trail-hardened blues — `#005e96` as the primary anchor, `#2055a3` as a deeper structural accent, and `#0a96cf` as the bright signal across navigation and interactive elements. The palette reads like a topographic map: cool, purposeful, and utterly unpretentious. Where other outdoor brands lean into earth tones or high-vis safety orange, Zpacks commits to a restrained navy-and-steel system punctuated by `#8da94e` (a muted sage-green that appears on category badges and environmental callouts) and `#e5fd6e` (a sharp chartreuse used sparingly for sale tags and urgency markers). The canvas is `#fdfdfd` — a near-white that avoids the sterile hospital feel of pure `#ffffff` — while `#f5f5f5` surfaces soft panels and card backgrounds. Hairlines run `#dedede`, keeping the grid present but quiet. The brand trusts its product photography (ultralight tents, frameless packs, titanium cookware against granite slabs) to carry the emotional weight; typography stays clean and unobtrusive, likely a system sans-serif at moderate weights. Buttons are rectangular with `{rounded.sm}` corners — no pill shapes, no whimsy. The search bar, however, goes full `{rounded.full}`, a subtle concession to usability on a site that otherwise feels engineered for the trail, not the mall. The overall effect is a brand that looks like it was designed by backpackers who happen to know CSS: every pixel justified by weight savings, every color chosen for legibility under a headlamp.

## Components

### Buttons
**`button-primary`** — The workhorse CTA of the Zpacks site, rendered in `#005e96` with white text and `{rounded.sm}` corners. On hover or active, it deepens to `#2055a3` (`button-primary-active`). The disabled state drops to `#bbbbbb`, signaling the button is non-functional. Padding is 12px vertical and 24px horizontal, giving it a compact but confident footprint at 44px height. **`button-secondary`** — An outlined variant with a white fill, `#005e96` text, and a 2px solid border matching the primary. Active state shifts the border and text to `#2055a3` with a `#f5f5f5` background. **`button-ghost`** — Transparent background, `#005e96` text, minimal padding (12px 16px). Used for text-style actions like "Clear filters" or "Cancel". **`button-sale`** — A high-visibility variant using `#e5fd6e` (chartreuse) background with dark `#50595e` text. Active state darkens to `#d4e45a`. Used exclusively for sale or clearance actions. **`button-cart`** — A sage-green (`#8da94e`) variant for add-to-cart flows. Active state deepens to `#7a9a42`. All buttons share the same 44px height and `{rounded.sm}` radius for visual consistency.

### Navigation
**`nav-bar`** — A fixed-height (64px) white bar with a single `#dedede` bottom border. On scroll, it gains a subtle `boxShadow` (`0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`) via `nav-bar-sticky`. **`nav-link`** — Inline links with 8px 12px padding and `{rounded.xs}` corners. Default state is `#50595e` text; hover adds a `#f5f5f5` background and shifts text to `#005e96`. The active state (`nav-link-active`) adds a 2px bottom border in `#005e96` to indicate the current section. **`search-bar`** — The only full-pill component (`{rounded.full}`) in the system, sitting at 48px height with a white background and `#dedede` border. On focus, the border thickens to 2px and turns `#005e96`. This pill shape is a deliberate usability concession — a single rounded form in an otherwise rectilinear system.

### Product Cards
**`product-card`** — A white card with `{rounded.md}` corners and a `#dedede` border. On hover, the border shifts to `#005e96` and a blue-tinted shadow appears (`0 4px 12px rgba(0,94,150,0.1)`). The image area uses `{rounded.md}` on top corners only. **`product-card-title`** — Uses `{typography.title-sm}` (14px, weight 600) with 8px horizontal and 12px top padding. **`product-card-price`** — Uses `{typography.price-sm}` (14px, weight 600) with 4px top and 16px bottom padding. **`product-card-badge`** — Three badge variants: default sage (`#8da94e`), sale chartreuse (`#e5fd6e` with dark text), and new teal (`#44a4b9`). All use `{typography.badge}` (11px uppercase, weight 700) with `{rounded.xs}` corners and 2px 8px padding.

### Forms & Inputs
**`text-input`** — Standard 44px height input with `{rounded.sm}` corners, white background, and `#dedede` border. Focus state thickens the border to 2px and turns it `#005e96`. Error state uses a 2px `#e14830` border. **`select`** — Matches text-input styling for visual consistency. **`quantity-selector`** — A compact 40px height control with `{rounded.sm}` corners and `#dedede` border. The increment/decrement buttons are 28px squares with `{rounded.xs}` corners and `#f5f5f5` background.

### Footer
**`footer-section`** — A dark `#50595e` background section with white text. Links use `{typography.link}` (14px, weight 500) in `#bbbbbb` with `{spacing.xs}` vertical padding. On hover, links brighten to `#fdfdfd`. The footer typically contains 3-4 columns of links, a newsletter signup, and brand copyright.

### Badges & Indicators
**`badge-default`** — Gray (`#f5f5f5` background, `#50595e` text) for neutral tags. **`badge-primary`** — Blue (`#005e96` background, white text) for featured or recommended items. **`badge-sale`** — Chartreuse (`#e5fd6e` background, `#50595e` text) for discounts. **`badge-new`** — Teal (`#44a4b9` background, white text) for new arrivals. **`badge-sold-out`** — Coral (`#e14830` background, white text) for out-of-stock items. All badges share `{typography.badge}` (11px uppercase, weight 700) with `{rounded.xs}` corners and 2px 8px padding.

### Navigation & Pagination
**`breadcrumb`** — A horizontal list of links in `{typography.caption}` (12px, weight 500). Default links are `#757577`; the active (current page) link is `#50595e`. Separators use `#dedede` with 4px horizontal padding. **`pagination-button`** — Standard 8px 12px buttons with `{rounded.sm}` corners and `#dedede` border. Active state fills with `#005e96` and white text. Disabled state uses `#f5f5f5` background with `#bbbbbb` text.

### Content Sections
**`hero-section`** — A full-width banner using `#005e96` background with white text and `{typography.display-xl}` (36px, weight 700). Minimum height of 400px with `{spacing.section}` (64px) vertical padding. ## Responsive Behavior

|---|---|---|

### Touch Targets
- All interactive elements (buttons, links, inputs) maintain minimum 44px height for touch accessibility
- Icon buttons are 40x40px with `{rounded.full}` corners
- Quantity selector buttons are 28x28px (minimum for tap targets)
- Search bar is 48px tall for easy thumb access
- Nav links have 8px 12px padding with 64px nav bar height providing ample tap area

### Collapsing Strategy
- Primary navigation collapses to hamburger menu below 744px
- Product grid collapses from 4 columns to 2 columns at tablet, 1 column at mobile
- Footer collapses from 4 columns to 2 columns at tablet, stacked at mobile
- Hero section reduces padding from 64px to 32px on mobile
- Category cards stack vertically on mobile, 2-up on tablet
- Accordion replaces tabbed content on mobile for product details and FAQs
- Search bar reduces from full-width to icon-only trigger on mobile, expanding to full overlay on tap
